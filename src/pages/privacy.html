Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

KINETIX GCC CV respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Information for your documents: nationality, location, visa status, work history, education, certificates, languages and, if you choose, date of birth, marital status and a photo.
  • Files you upload: certificates, existing CVs and other documents, including anything you attach to a free help ticket.
  • Order details: service, price, payment method and order status. We do not collect or store card numbers.

Why we use it

To prepare and deliver your documents, contact you about your order, take payment, give you order tracking and revisions, and keep records of our work. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Your data is stored in our Google Drive and Google Sheets accounts and processed with Google Apps Script and Google email services. Order alerts are sent to our team by WhatsApp message and email. Your files are visible only to us. We share information with others only when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

Free PDF tools

Our PDF tools (fill & sign, signature maker, password remover, merge/split and the rest) run entirely in your own browser. The PDF or photo you open in them is never uploaded to us or to any server - it stays on your device the whole time, including when you use the camera to photograph a signature or ID photo, the crop and rotate tools, or the text-detection (OCR) tool. The only exception is the free help ticket, which does send your file to us so our team can act on it by hand, the same as any other order.

Offline mode and your device

If you allow it, the free PDF tool pages can be saved on your device so they still open with a weak signal. This only stores the tool's own code, not your files or documents. You can turn this off, or clear it, from the prompt shown on those pages or from your browser's site data settings.

Video tutorials

Tutorial videos are plain video files. If you download one, that happens directly in your browser; we don't track who downloads what.

Storage and deletion

We keep order records and files for as long as needed to support revisions and our records, then delete them on request. To ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data, message us on WhatsApp or email with your reference number.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores your light or dark choice, an unfinished order form so you do not lose it, your last order reference, and - only if you allow it - the offline copy of the free PDF tools described above. You can clear these in your browser settings.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service is completely secure. Please do not send passwords or card numbers.
