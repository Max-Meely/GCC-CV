Documents to prepare before you apply for a Gulf job

Key points
  • Keep clear scans, not dark phone photos.
  • Write your work history with exact employers and dates.
  • Name files clearly so they can be sent in seconds.
In this articleThe basic checklistHow to scan wellName your filesWrite down your work history

Recruiters often ask for documents on the same day they call you. People who have everything ready in one folder get the next step first.

The basic checklist

  • Passport copy: photo page and visa page if you have one
  • Recent passport-style photo with a plain background
  • Certificates, trade tests and licences
  • Experience or reference letters from past employers
  • Education certificates
  • Driving licence, if the job needs driving
  • Emirates ID or national ID copy, where you have it

How to scan well

  1. Use good light and place the document flat on a table.
  2. Use a scanning app so the page is cropped straight and readable.
  3. Check every name, number and date is sharp.
  4. Save as PDF when a document has more than one page.

Name your files

Use names like Passport-Samuel-Otieno.pdf and H2S-Awareness-2025.pdf, not "IMG_20261103.jpg". It saves the recruiter time and makes you look organised.

Write down your work history

Before you start a CV, list each job: employer, project or site, city, job title, start and end month. Gaps are far easier to explain when the dates are accurate.
