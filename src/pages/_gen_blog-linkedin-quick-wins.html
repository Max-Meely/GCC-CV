LinkedIn quick wins for GCC job seekers

Key points
  • Your headline should say your trade and location.
  • Write a short About section with your strengths.
  • Turn on Open to Work for recruiters only.

Many Gulf recruiters search LinkedIn before they post an advert. A clear profile can bring messages to you.

Fix these first

  1. Photo: a clear, friendly, recent photo with a plain background.
  2. Headline: your trade and location, for example "Electrical Technician | UAE | Available immediately".
  3. About: four or five lines on your experience, your sector and what you are looking for.
  4. Experience: the same jobs, titles and dates as your CV.
  5. Skills: add the tools and standards you use, and ask colleagues to endorse them.
  6. Open to Work: choose the option that only recruiters can see.

Keep it consistent

Your LinkedIn titles and dates must match your CV and certificates. Recruiters compare them.
