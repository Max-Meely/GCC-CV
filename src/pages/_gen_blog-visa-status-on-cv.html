How to show your visa status on a CV

Key points
  • Always state your status. Do not leave it blank.
  • Be honest about dates, such as visit visa expiry or grace period.
  • Add when you can start.

Employers in the Gulf need to know if they can hire you quickly. A clear visa line saves everyone time and builds trust.

Simple wording examples

  • Employment visa: "Visa: Valid UAE employment visa | Available: Immediately"
  • Visit visa: "Visa: Visit visa, valid until 20 Dec 2026 | Available: Immediately"
  • Cancelled visa: "Visa: Cancelled, within grace period until 15 Dec 2026"
  • Outside the UAE: "Location: Nairobi, Kenya | Available: 2 weeks after job offer"

Why dates matter

A visit visa or grace period has an end date. Including it shows you understand the timeline and lets the recruiter plan. Rules about grace periods and visa changes can change, so confirm current rules with your PRO, employer or the official immigration channels.

Be careful. Never claim a visa status you do not have. Employers check, and false information can end an application at once.
