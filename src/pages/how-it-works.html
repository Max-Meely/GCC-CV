The 6 steps

  1. Package and style. Choose a package, add a matching cover letter if you need one, and pick your CV style.
  2. About you. Name as on your passport, phone or WhatsApp, email, nationality and where you are now.
  3. The job you want. Pick your industry and role. We add the skills, duties and a professional summary for you. Choose your visa status, including ready for demobilisation.
  4. Work history. Add your jobs, newest first. Leave the duties empty and we write them. Freshers can skip this step.
  5. Education and certificates. List your certificates, choose the order they appear in and add photos or scans if you want.
  6. Review and pay. Check the total, choose M-Pesa, Equity Bank or cash, agree to the terms and press Submit.

Common questions

ATS or designed style?

An ATS (applicant tracking system) is software that reads and sorts CVs before a person does. ATS styles are plain on purpose so it can read every word. Use them for online applications and recruitment agencies. Designed styles use colour and a sidebar and look sharper, so use them when you send your CV straight to a person. Not sure? Choose ATS.

How long will I wait?

You get your reference number and a confirmation email right away. Documents are usually ready within 24 to 48 hours of your payment being confirmed. You can follow progress on the Track order page.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. One minor revision is free for 3 days after delivery. Extra revisions are AED 15 each. Use the revision request page with your reference number.

What about LinkedIn?

LinkedIn Basic (AED 50) rewrites your headline, About section and experience. LinkedIn Full (AED 100) adds the full profile, skills and a keyword strategy. Recruiters search by keywords, so the right words help you get found. It is also part of the Full Career Package.

What should I have ready?

Your passport details, employer names with dates, your certificates and licences, and a recent photo if you choose a designed style. Phone photos are fine and are shrunk on your phone before sending.

How do I pay?

Payment is 100% upfront and no extra charges are added on any method. After you submit, send your proof of payment on WhatsApp with your reference number.
