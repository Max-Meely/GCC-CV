How to read a job advert and match your CV to it

Key points
  • Separate must-have requirements from nice-to-have ones.
  • Use the advert job title if it matches your experience.
  • Send the CV that fits this job, not a generic one.
In this articleStep 1: find the must-havesStep 2: match your CV honestlyStep 3: check the detailsStep 4: reply the right way

Every job advert is a small instruction sheet. Read it twice before you send a CV.

Step 1: find the must-haves

Look for words like required, must have, minimum. These usually decide whether your CV is considered. Examples are years of experience, a trade test, a licence or a safety certificate.

Step 2: match your CV honestly

  • Use the same job title if it truly matches your work.
  • Mention the same tools, standards or equipment where you have used them.
  • Move the most relevant certificates and jobs near the top.

Step 3: check the details

  • Where to send the application, and in what file type
  • Closing date and any reference number to quote
  • Whether a visa, walk-in or interview date is mentioned

Step 4: reply the right way

Put the job title or reference in the email subject. Write two short lines in the email, attach your CV, and keep your phone on.

Tip. Keep a master list of your jobs, duties and certificates. For each application, copy only what fits that advert.
