10 CV mistakes that get Gulf applications rejected

Key points
  • Most rejections come from simple, fixable mistakes.
  • Accuracy and clarity beat decoration.
  • Always proofread before sending.

A CV rarely fails because of one big problem. It fails because of several small ones that tell a recruiter you are not careful. Check yours against this list.

  1. No clear job title. Say what you do: "Electrical Job Performer", not "Seeking opportunities".
  2. Missing contact details. A phone number without WhatsApp, or a wrong email.
  3. No visa status or availability. Employers must guess.
  4. Long paragraphs of duties. Use short bullet points.
  5. Duties that are all the same for every job. Be specific to each role.
  6. Dates that do not match your documents.
  7. An unprofessional email address. Use your name.
  8. Heavy design that breaks screening software.
  9. Spelling mistakes, especially in your own job title and company names.
  10. Too long. More than three pages almost never gets read.
Tip. Ask a friend to read your CV for 30 seconds and tell you what job you want and where you work now. If they cannot, a recruiter will not be able to either.
