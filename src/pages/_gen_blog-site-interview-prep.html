Preparing for a site or camp interview

Key points
  • Know your own CV by heart: dates, sites and duties.
  • Be ready to explain safety basics in simple words.
  • Arrive on time and carry your documents.
In this articleQuestions you may hearHow to prepareOn the day

Interviews for site, trade and technician jobs are usually short. They test three things: do you really have the experience, are you safe, and can you work with a team.

Questions you may hear

  • "Tell me about your last job. What did you do every day?"
  • "Which sites or projects have you worked on?"
  • "What safety training do you have?"
  • "What would you do if you saw an unsafe condition?"
  • "Can you start now? Do you need a visa?"

How to prepare

  1. Read your own CV again and be ready to explain every line.
  2. Practise answering in two or three simple sentences.
  3. Learn basic safety words for your trade: PPE, permit to work, toolbox talk, housekeeping.
  4. Carry original and copy documents in a clean folder.
  5. Ask when you can start and about transport, accommodation and pay dates.

On the day

Arrive early, dress neatly, switch your phone to silent and be polite to everyone you meet, from the guard to the HR officer.

Tip. Keep your answers true. If you do not know something, say you will learn it. Honesty is respected on site.
