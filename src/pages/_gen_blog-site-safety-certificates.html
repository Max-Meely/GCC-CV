Safety certificates site employers often ask about

Key points
  • Requirements depend on the employer and the project.
  • List name, issuer and year for every certificate.
  • Keep the original certificates and scans safe and readable.
In this articleCommon onesHow to list them on your CVKeep them valid

Large industrial sites in the region often expect workers to hold basic safety training before they start. Exact requirements depend on the employer, the client and the project, so always confirm with the company. These are the certificates most commonly mentioned.

Common ones

  • H2S awareness: hydrogen sulphide hazards and response
  • ADNOC WMS or HSE induction: for ADNOC-related work
  • Basic safety induction: site rules, PPE and emergency procedures
  • Working at height: fall prevention and safe access
  • Confined space awareness: entry hazards and permits
  • Permit to work awareness: how permits control risk
  • First aid and CPR, fire fighting, banksman, rigger and slinger for specific roles

How to list them on your CV

Create a section called Certificates and Licences. For each one write the name, the training provider or issuer, and the year. Keep the newest at the top.

Keep them valid

Many certificates expire. Note the expiry date in your own records and renew before it passes, so an employer never finds you holding an out-of-date card.

Be careful. Never list a certificate you do not hold. Sites verify cards, and a false claim can cost you the job and future work.
