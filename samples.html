Which style should I choose?

  • ATS & Technical - plain layout that company systems read cleanly. Best for oil and gas, construction and technical jobs.
  • Designed - a colour header and clear sections for a stronger first look. Good when a person reads the CV.
  • Side panel - skills and contact in a coloured column, experience on the right. Good for hospitality, retail and office jobs.
