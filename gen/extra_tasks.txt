# Extra duties so every role has 12 (a CV needs at least 10 per job).  Format:  + Role | task; task; task; task
# Core competencies per industry (8):  C@key: a; b; c ...

C@construction: Site safety awareness; Reading drawings; Quality workmanship; Productivity on site; Following method statements; Teamwork; Time management; Use of tools and equipment
C@oilgas: Permit to work compliance; HSE awareness; Shutdown and turnaround support; Technical drawing interpretation; Quality and inspection readiness; Working at height and confined space; Teamwork in high-risk areas; Reliability
C@hse: Risk assessment; Incident investigation; Permit to work control; Safety inspections and audits; Emergency preparedness; Regulatory compliance; Training and coaching; Clear reporting
C@factory: Production efficiency; Quality control; Machine operation; Preventive maintenance; Lean and waste reduction; Health and safety; Shift reporting; Teamwork
C@corporate: Administration and coordination; Records and document control; Confidentiality; Communication; Organisation and planning; Attention to detail; Customer focus; Computer literacy
C@healthcare: Patient care; Infection control; Accuracy and documentation; Compassion and communication; Emergency response; Confidentiality; Teamwork; Following protocols
C@hospitality: Guest service; Food safety and hygiene; Speed and accuracy; Upselling; Teamwork in service; Cleanliness standards; Complaint handling; Flexibility with shifts
C@security: Access control; Patrol and observation; Incident reporting; Emergency response; Alertness; Report writing; Customer courtesy; Discipline and reliability
C@retail: Customer service; Sales and target achievement; Stock replenishment; Cash handling; Product knowledge; Visual merchandising; Communication; Teamwork
C@facilities: Preventive maintenance; Fault finding; Safe working practices; Reading drawings and diagrams; Response to breakdowns; Record keeping; Customer courtesy; Reliability

+ Warehouse Assistant | Break down and dispose of packaging materials and cartons in line with site rules; Assist with inventory audits and cycle counts and recount items when asked; Prepare returns and damaged goods for inspection and disposal; Support the team during peak periods and urgent dispatches
+ Storekeeper | Prepare material receiving reports and match them to invoices and delivery notes; Segregate and tag damaged, expired or non-conforming materials; Update the material register and issue logs for audits and inspections; Return surplus site materials to store and update the balances
+ Forklift Operator | Position loads accurately on racking and floor locations to the warehouse layout; Work with pickers and dispatch staff to meet loading deadlines; Keep pedestrian walkways clear and use horn and lights as required; Report near misses, collisions or damage to the supervisor straight away
+ Inventory Controller | Prepare cycle count schedules and assign counts to store teams; Check goods received notes against purchase orders before posting stock; Review stock adjustments and obtain approvals before they are posted; Support audits by providing stock records, count sheets and variance explanations
+ Warehouse Supervisor | Plan the layout and bin allocation to use space efficiently; Handle shift handovers and make sure open orders are cleared on time; Inspect forklifts, racking and equipment and arrange repairs; Prepare daily productivity and safety reports for the warehouse manager
+ Picker / Packer | Replenish pick faces from reserve locations when stock runs low; Use handheld scanners to confirm each item and location before picking; Attach correct labels, barcodes and handling instructions to parcels; Carry out spot checks on completed orders to reduce errors
+ Delivery Driver | Confirm delivery addresses and contact customers before arrival; Keep accurate delivery logs and submit paperwork at the end of each shift; Handle returns and collect damaged or refused goods; Report accidents, delays and route problems to dispatch immediately
+ Logistics Coordinator | Book transport and negotiate rates with trucking and freight companies; Update the tracking sheet and notify customers of expected delivery times; Coordinate with warehouse teams on loading schedules and cut-off times; Prepare weekly reports on on-time delivery and shipment status

+ General Labourer | Clear debris and waste from work areas and load it for disposal; Help fix and strip formwork under the supervision of carpenters; Wet and cure concrete after pouring as instructed; Move materials by trolley, wheelbarrow or hoist where needed
+ Mason | Install block work reinforcement, damp-proof courses and joint fillers; Cure finished plaster and block work to avoid cracking; Check levels and measurements before handing over each section; Estimate material quantities and report shortages to the foreman
+ Carpenter / Shuttering | Set out formwork positions from surveyed lines and levels; Apply release agent and seal joints to keep concrete finishes clean; Assemble and install temporary supports and props safely; Check and secure formwork before concrete pours with the engineer
+ Steel Fixer | Fix starter bars, laps and links to the specified lengths and spacing; Prepare rebar cages and lift them into position with the rigging team; Check cover, spacing and bar sizes with the engineer before pouring; Record steel used and report off-cuts and wastage
+ Scaffolder | Prepare scaffold drawings and material lists before each erection; Install guardrails, toe boards, ladders and access gates to standard; Update scaffold registers and display inspection tags at each lift; Dismantle scaffolds safely from the top down using the correct sequence
+ Site Foreman | Prepare daily and weekly work plans and share them with the crew; Check quality on completed work and organise corrections quickly; Report manpower, equipment and material needs ahead of time; Hold toolbox talks and make sure permits are in place before work begins
+ Civil Engineer | Monitor concrete pours, curing, and test results with the quality team; Prepare daily and weekly reports with photos and progress updates; Check subcontractor work and prepare payment certificates; Maintain as-built records and support handover documents
+ Surveyor | Set out grids, foundations, columns and levels for each work stage; Check the verticality and alignment of completed structures; Process survey data into drawings and quantity reports; Provide levels and layout support to engineers and foremen daily
+ Heavy Equipment Operator | Load trucks safely and keep to the haul routes and speed limits; Grade surfaces to the required levels and gradients; Record fuel use, working hours and defects in the equipment log; Work near underground services and live areas only with permits

+ Helper / Job Performer | Hold the permit board and keep records of tools used in the work area; Help set up barriers, signage and access routes around the job; Prepare materials for pipe, mechanical and painting crews; Clean tools and return them to the tool store after each job
+ Rigger | Check the weight, centre of gravity and rigging method before each lift; Use tag lines and communication methods to control loads; Inspect the lifting area and remove hazards before work starts; Support heavy equipment and module installation under supervision
+ Pipe Fitter | Mark, cut and install pipe supports and hangers to the drawings; Carry out dimensional checks on spools and installed lines; Assist with flushing, cleaning and pressure testing of pipelines; Keep fitting records, marked-up drawings and material logs up to date
+ Welder (6G) | Preheat and control interpass temperature when required by the WPS; Weld branch connections, nozzles and structural attachments; Maintain welding records and identify joints with the welder ID; Support radiography and NDT by repairing defects as instructed
+ Mechanical Technician | Replace seals, bearings, couplings and gaskets on rotating equipment; Check vibration, temperature and lubrication on running machines; Prepare equipment for isolation and hand it over for repair; Fill in job cards, equipment history and spare parts requests
+ Instrument Technician | Install tubing, impulse lines and instrument supports to the drawings; Test alarms, trips and interlocks with the control room; Maintain calibration devices and test equipment; Prepare instrument punch lists and close out items before handover
+ Painter / Sandblaster | Mask and protect nearby equipment and surfaces before blasting or painting; Check blasting profile and surface cleanliness before coating; Keep records of paint batches, thickness and inspection results; Touch up damage and repair coatings after installation work
+ Insulator | Fit removable insulation covers and blankets on valves and flanges; Install aluminium or stainless cladding with correct overlaps and sealing; Measure and mark pipe and vessel surfaces to cut materials accurately; Inspect completed insulation with the supervisor and fix any defects
+ Crane Operator / Banksman | Check outriggers, ground bearing and set-up before any lift; Keep the crane and radio equipment clean and in good working order; Maintain clear communication with the lift supervisor throughout operations; Stop lifts at once when weather or site conditions become unsafe
+ QA/QC Inspector | Inspect fabrication, fit-up and final welds against drawings and codes; Witness hydrotests, pressure tests and equipment commissioning; Check material receiving and identify non-conforming items; Prepare daily inspection reports and close out punch list items
+ Planner | Prepare look-ahead schedules and track key milestones weekly; Update progress curves and earned value reports for management; Review contractor schedules and highlight conflicts; Maintain records of schedule changes, delays and approvals

+ HSE Officer | Monitor housekeeping, waste and environmental controls on site; Check the condition of tools, equipment and access ways; Conduct pre-task briefings and observe high-risk work; Maintain training matrices and certificate records for workers
+ Safety Supervisor | Lead daily safety walkdowns and record findings and actions; Supervise high-risk activities including hot work, lifting and confined space; Mentor safety officers and coordinate shift handovers; Review near-miss and observation trends and suggest preventive actions
+ Fire Watch / Marshal | Check area clearances and remove combustible materials before work begins; Remain in position for the required time after hot work finishes; Communicate with the permit issuer, crew and control room as needed; Wear the correct PPE and keep a fire extinguisher ready at all times
+ Permit Receiver | Review job scope and control measures with the crew before starting; Confirm tools, materials and PPE are suitable for the job; Report changes in scope or conditions to the permit issuer; Keep the work area safe and tidy during and after the job
+ Medic / First Aider | Carry out pre-employment and routine medical screening where required; Monitor workers for heat stress, fatigue and health risks; Keep emergency response equipment ready and in date; Provide health talks and support wellbeing campaigns for the workforce

+ Machine Operator | Change tools, dies and settings as the production plan requires; Inspect raw materials and finished products against the specification; Complete production reports and downtime records at the end of each shift; Keep the work area tidy and follow 5S standards
+ Production Supervisor | Review daily output against plan and adjust line staffing; Resolve quality or safety problems and report root causes; Coordinate shift handovers and communicate priorities clearly; Support continuous improvement and waste-reduction activities
+ QC Inspector | Sample products at set intervals and record measurements accurately; Quarantine non-conforming products and tag them clearly; Check packaging, labelling and traceability on finished goods; Prepare quality reports and support corrective action plans
+ Packaging Operator | Change over packing materials and clean machines between batches; Weigh and check product counts against batch records; Load pallets, wrap them and apply labels correctly; Support line clearance before new orders start
+ Maintenance Technician | Inspect belts, chains, motors and sensors during planned stops; Fault-find electrical panels and control circuits with testers; Carry out minor welding, fitting and fabrication repairs; Assist with commissioning, trials and handover of repaired machines

+ Admin Assistant | Prepare minutes, travel documents and booking confirmations; Maintain staff attendance sheets and office records; Coordinate courier, transport and maintenance requests; Draft routine emails and keep contact lists up to date
+ Document Controller | Check documents for correct numbering, naming and revision status; Issue documents to teams and keep distribution records; Maintain the master document register and project folders; Support audits and handover with complete document packages
+ Accountant | Reconcile bank, supplier and customer accounts each month; Prepare cost reports and cash flow updates for management; Check expense claims, payment vouchers and approvals; Maintain fixed asset registers and depreciation schedules
+ HR Officer | Prepare joining formalities, ID cards and medical and insurance documents; Track visa and labour card renewals and passport records; Support exit formalities, final settlements and clearance forms; Prepare HR reports on headcount, attendance and turnover
+ Receptionist | Manage courier and mail logs and notify staff of arrivals; Coordinate with security for visitor access and parking; Update the staff directory and phone extension lists; Support events, meetings and catering arrangements
+ Procurement Officer | Raise purchase orders and track them until goods are received; Evaluate suppliers on price, quality and delivery time; Handle supplier invoices and resolve payment queries with finance; Maintain procurement trackers, price comparisons and contract files
+ Customer Service Representative | Log customer requests and keep them updated until closure; Process orders, bookings and cancellations accurately; Review call quality and learn from supervisor feedback; Prepare daily reports on enquiries, complaints and resolution time

+ Nurse | Prepare patients for procedures and monitor them afterwards; Perform wound care, dressings and basic nursing procedures; Hand over patients at shift change with clear notes; Maintain ward stock, equipment and medicine storage
+ Caregiver | Assist patients with mobility, transfers and exercises; Help patients take part in activities and keep to daily routines; Keep patients' rooms, clothing and linen clean; Report falls, pain or behaviour changes to nurses and families
+ Laboratory Technician | Prepare slides, samples and reagents for testing; Calibrate equipment and run daily quality control checks; Enter results in the laboratory system and flag critical values; Follow chain-of-custody and sample storage procedures
+ Pharmacy Assistant | Stock shelves and rotate products by expiry date; Prepare repeat prescriptions for pharmacist verification; Advise customers on over-the-counter products within scope; Handle returns, damaged goods and expired stock correctly

+ Waiter / Waitress | Explain menu items, ingredients and allergens to guests; Prepare side stations, cutlery and glassware before service; Handle special requests and celebrations with care; Support closing duties, cleaning and restocking at the end of the shift
+ Housekeeping Attendant | Clean and restock public areas, corridors and lifts; Inspect rooms after cleaning to meet brand standards; Handle laundry, linen counts and guest requests; Check minibars and report consumption accurately
+ Cook / Commis Chef | Wash, peel, cut and portion vegetables, meat and fish; Maintain labels, dates and storage temperatures on all food; Follow recipes, portion sizes and plating guides; Clean ovens, grills, fryers and work surfaces after service
+ Barista | Grind, dose and tamp coffee consistently for the best taste; Steam and pour milk to create latte art and quality drinks; Prepare cold drinks, teas and other menu items; Open and close the shop, count the till and restock supplies
+ Front Desk Agent | Handle phone, email and walk-in reservations; Manage room allocations and special guest requests; Handle foreign currency, deposits and billing queries; Coordinate wake-up calls, luggage and transport requests

+ Security Guard | Operate metal detectors, scanners and access barriers; Record visitor details and issue and collect passes; Inspect fire exits, locks and alarm systems on patrol; Escort visitors and staff where required by site rules
+ CCTV Operator | Track suspects and incidents across cameras and zones; Report system faults and equipment problems quickly; Prepare daily shift and incident summaries; Test alarms and cameras at the start of each shift
+ Security Supervisor | Prepare post orders and update them with client requirements; Manage shift handovers, patrols and incident follow-up; Check uniforms, equipment and licences of guards; Investigate breaches and recommend improvements

+ Sales Associate | Greet walk-in customers and offer help promptly; Keep up to date with new products, offers and promotions; Handle product demonstrations and fitting assistance; Tidy fitting rooms and restock items throughout the day
+ Cashier | Process gift cards, vouchers and loyalty points; Check cash and card receipts at the end of each shift; Handle price queries and refer problems to supervisors; Keep the till and counter clean and stocked with supplies
+ Store Supervisor | Prepare staff schedules and cover absences; Check cash, banking and daily sales reports; Handle stock deliveries, price changes and promotions; Coach team members and give feedback on performance
+ Merchandiser | Check promotions, pricing and planograms in each store; Report out-of-stock items and arrange quick replenishment; Update visit records and share them with sales managers; Present products attractively to boost customer visibility

+ Electrician | Install and test earthing, protection devices and distribution boards; Replace lamps, fittings and emergency lights on schedule; Carry out inspection and testing of installations and record readings; Respond to power faults and restore supply safely
+ AC / HVAC Technician | Check refrigerant levels and leak test pipework; Clean drain lines and replace worn belts and filters; Measure airflow, temperature and electrical load; Prepare customer reports and recommend repairs
+ Plumber | Install taps, mixers, toilets and drainage fittings; Check water pressure, pumps and tanks regularly; Repair burst or leaking pipes and restore supply quickly; Support preventive maintenance on plumbing systems
+ Cleaner | Clean glass, walls and high-touch surfaces with the correct products; Operate polishing and scrubbing machines safely; Handle waste segregation and recycling; Report spills and slip hazards at once
+ Light Vehicle Driver | Collect and drop staff at agreed times and locations; Keep the vehicle documents, insurance and registration valid; Follow safe parking, reversing and passenger-loading rules; Report accidents, fines and mechanical problems promptly
+ Handyman / Maintenance Helper | Paint walls, patch holes and repair simple fittings; Replace light bulbs, locks, hinges and door fittings; Support electricians, plumbers and AC technicians when needed; Keep stock of common spare parts and report shortages
